Autumn colours in the Cairngorms and some news from us

 

Dear friends of Garden Park,

the glens are turning gold, the evenings are getting quieter, and the fire in the lounge is lit again. Here is a short update on what has been happening at Garden Park guest house, and what awaits you on your next stay with us. We want Garden Park to be more than just a bed for the night. We want it to be a place you’ll want to come back to.

We also believe that Grantown-on-Spey is one of the very best bases for exploring the Scottish Highlands. With so many incredible places within easy reach, we created an interactive map to help you discover our favourite walks, castles, whisky distilleries, wildlife, restaurants and hidden gems.

Dinner, not at Garden Park Guest House

While we don’t serve evening meals, we’d still like to offer you a delicious option.

From Monday to Saturday, we freshly prepare our popular Wee Fancy Brügel, a generous 30 cm (approx. 380 g) Swiss-style gourmet sandwiches made with carefully selected, high-quality ingredients.

Many of our ingredients are sourced locally, including freshly prepared meats from our trusted butcher in Nethy Bridge. To ensure everything is as fresh as possible, we kindly ask you to place your order at breakfast.

Generation Getaway

Three Generations. One Memory.

Some of the most precious childhood memories are made with grandparents.

Strathspey & Badenoch, the Cairngorms and the Moray Coast offer the perfect setting for a family staycation filled with nature, adventure and quality time together.

Based in Grantown-on-Spey, you’ll be perfectly located for relaxed day trips, scenic walks, wildlife encounters and unforgettable family moments.

Our Generation Getaway

Our unique concept brings three generations together while giving everyone the space to enjoy their holiday.

Grandparents stay with up to two grandchildren (up to 10 years old) in our cosy Butterfly Cottage* – a place for bedtime stories, board games and peaceful evenings after exciting days exploring the Highlands.

Parents stay just a few steps away in our comfortable Grant Room*, featuring a spacious modern bathroom with a luxurious 1.8 m rainfall shower – the perfect place to unwind and enjoy some well-deserved time together.

Every morning begins with a freshly prepared breakfast at Garden Park Guest House.

Every evening is about sharing stories, planning the next day’s adventure and creating memories that will last a lifetime in the cosy guest lounge we’ve created for our guests.
